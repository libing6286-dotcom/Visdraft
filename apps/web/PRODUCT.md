# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Visdraft serves AI design, video, and visual-content creators who want to move from idea to finished creative work without learning prompt engineering, timeline editing, or template systems. It also serves teams and developers who care about open-source ownership, self-hosting, data control, and flexible model-provider access.

## Product Purpose

Visdraft is an AI creative workspace built around an infinite canvas and natural-language collaboration. Users describe what they want, generate images and videos, arrange and edit assets on the canvas, and manage brand assets so repeated creative output stays consistent. Success means a user can complete the full creative flow, from rough intent to exportable work, inside one workspace.

## Positioning

Visdraft is the open-source, self-hostable AI creative canvas that replaces templates and timelines with conversation.

## Brand Personality

Professional, open, and creator-friendly. The interface should feel capable enough for serious creative work while staying approachable for users who want to think in ideas instead of tooling.

## Anti-references

Visdraft should not feel like a closed SaaS template tool, a traditional timeline-first editing suite, or a flashy but untrustworthy AI generator. Avoid surfaces that sell spectacle at the expense of control, clarity, or ownership.

## Design Principles

Keep the canvas as the center of gravity: navigation, chat, generation controls, and brand tools should support creative flow instead of competing with it.

Make AI legible and steerable: users should understand what is happening, what can be changed, and how to recover from failed or partial generation.

Favor creator control over magic: automation should accelerate the work, but final editing, placement, brand consistency, and export decisions must remain visible and direct.

Earn trust through restraint: product UI should be consistent, readable, and task-focused; expressive moments belong where they clarify creative possibility.

Respect ownership: language and workflows should reinforce that users can self-host, choose providers, and keep their data under their control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for product surfaces. Maintain readable contrast, visible focus states, keyboard access for primary workflows, touch targets that hold up on mobile web, and reduced-motion alternatives for non-essential animation.
